Privacy Policy for SwipeCleaner

Last updated: August 22, 2026

SwipeCleaner is a photo library management app for iOS. This policy explains what information the app accesses and what it does with it.

Photo library access

SwipeCleaner asks for access to your photo library so you can review and delete photos and videos from within the app. Your photos are processed entirely on your device. SwipeCleaner does not upload, transmit, or share your photos or videos with the developer or with any third party.

Location

If a photo includes location information, SwipeCleaner may show a place name (for example, "Madrid, Spain") next to it. To do this, the app sends the photo's coordinates to Apple's location services to resolve a place name. This lookup is governed by Apple's own privacy practices; SwipeCleaner does not store, log, or have access to this location data beyond displaying the resulting place name to you.

Accounts and data storage

SwipeCleaner does not require creating an account or signing in. The record of which photos you've already reviewed is stored locally on your device only — it is never synced to iCloud or to any server, and is not accessible to the developer.

Analytics and advertising

SwipeCleaner does not use any analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

In-app purchases

SwipeCleaner may offer an optional paid tier ("Pro") through Apple's standard in-app purchase system. Any payment is handled entirely by Apple; SwipeCleaner does not receive or store your payment information. Purchase and subscription status may be processed through RevenueCat, a subscription-management service, solely to confirm your entitlement — see RevenueCat's own privacy policy for details on how they handle this.

Children's privacy

SwipeCleaner is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

Contact
