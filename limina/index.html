Privacy Policy for Limina

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Limina is a photo library management app for iOS. This policy explains what information the app accesses and what it does with it.

Photo library access

Limina asks for access to your photo library so you can review and delete photos and videos from within the app. Your photos are processed entirely on your device. Limina does not upload, transmit, or share your photos or videos with the developer or with any third party.

Location

If a photo includes location information, Limina may show a place name (for example, "Madrid, Spain") next to it. To do this, the app sends the photo's coordinates to Apple's location services to resolve a place name. This lookup is governed by Apple's own privacy practices; Limina does not store, log, or have access to this location data beyond displaying the resulting place name to you.

Accounts and data storage

Limina does not require creating an account or signing in. The record of which photos you've already reviewed is stored locally on your device only — it is never synced to iCloud or to any server, and is not accessible to the developer.

Advertising (free version)

The free version of Limina shows ads. These are served by Google AdMob, and the app includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to deliver them and to record consent. The Pro tier removes ads entirely; for Pro users the app makes no ad requests.

Ads are non-personalized by default. They only become personalized if both of these are true:

  • you allow tracking when iOS shows Limina's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and
  • where local law requires it (the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), you also give consent in the Google UMP form.

When ads are personalized, Google and its advertising partners may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) together with ad-interaction data and your coarse (non-precise) location to select and measure the ads you see. Under Apple's definition this is "tracking", and under some United States state privacy laws it is a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. Details of what Google receives and how it is used are in Google's "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services".

You can decline, and nothing in the app stops working. If you decline, ads stay non-personalized. You can change your choice at any time:

  • tracking — iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking;
  • EEA / UK / Switzerland consent — the "Privacy options" row in Limina's Settings screen, which reopens the Google UMP form;
  • United States state privacy rights (including the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information) — the same "Privacy options" row.

Limina also uses RevenueCat to attribute advertising revenue in aggregate, so the developer can see how much ad income the app generates. This does not add any identifier beyond what Google already processes for the ad itself.

Analytics

Limina does not use any analytics SDK and does not build a profile of how you use the app.

In-app purchases

Limina may offer an optional paid tier ("Pro") through Apple's standard in-app purchase system. Any payment is handled entirely by Apple; Limina does not receive or store your payment information. Purchase and subscription status may be processed through RevenueCat, a subscription-management service, solely to confirm your entitlement — see RevenueCat's own privacy policy for details on how they handle this.

Children's privacy

Limina is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
