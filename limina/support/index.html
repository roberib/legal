Limina Support

Help with the photo tidying app for iOS.

Limina shows you your photos in small batches so you can decide which to keep. If something isn't working or you have a question, the answers below cover the most common cases — anything else, email us.

How deletion works

Nothing is deleted while you review. Photos you mark are removed only after you confirm on the summary screen, and they go to your Recently Deleted album, where iOS keeps them for 30 days before they are permanently erased. You can recover them from the Photos app during that window.

I marked a photo by mistake

On the summary screen, before you confirm, you can keep any photo you marked. After confirming, open the Photos app, go to Recently Deleted, and restore it there.

Restoring a purchase

If you bought the optional Pro tier and it isn't showing on a device, open Limina's settings and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not in the app. On your device, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or the App Store's account page, to change or cancel.
