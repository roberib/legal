Pose Battle Support

A camera pose game for one or two players, on iPhone and iPhone Duo.

How do I play?

Pose Battle has three ways to play. In The Guide one player sees the target pose and describes it with words only, while the other poses blind in front of the camera with a cold-to-hot thermometer as the only clue. In Verified Mime one player acts out a secret pose, the camera checks it, and the other chooses which one it was among four. Blitz is solo against the clock: 30, 60 or 90 seconds, and every pose you nail brings the next.

Where do I stand?

Prop the iPhone up or hold it so the camera sees you from head to knees, a couple of steps away, with the room lit from the front. The app tells you when it can see you well enough; if your arms don't fit, step back.

The camera doesn't see me, or the poses don't register

Check that Pose Battle has camera access in Settings › Pose Battle › Camera, wipe the lens, and avoid a bright window behind you. Plain clothes that contrast with the wall work best.

Can I play with two iPhones?

Yes. Both must be on the same Wi-Fi (or have Bluetooth on) and allow the local network when the app asks. One iPhone is propped up as the camera; the other is held by the guide or guesser. On an iPhone Duo, open the phone and each player gets a screen. Nothing goes through the Internet.

Is the camera recording me?

No. Images are analysed on the iPhone to follow your pose and discarded right away; they are never stored and never leave the device. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Restoring a purchase

If you bought the Full Game and a device doesn't show it, open Full Game from the home screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Where are my records?

Your records are kept on this iPhone, without an account, so deleting the app resets them. The Blitz also has world leaderboards in Game Center if you are signed in to it.
