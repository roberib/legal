Privacy Policy for Pose Battle

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Pose Battle has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. What you do in the game stays on your iPhone.

What is stored on your iPhone

The names you type for the players, your records and games, the poses you record and your settings. All of it lives in the app's storage and is deleted with the app.

The camera

Images are analyzed on the iPhone to track your pose and discarded right away: they are never stored and never leave the device. Of the poses you record, only the skeleton points are kept, never a photo.

Two iPhones

When you play with another iPhone, the two find each other over the local network and exchange names, stick-figure points and scores; never images. Nothing goes through the Internet.

Game Center and purchases

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple manages your account: the app only receives your alias and sends your scores to the leaderboard. Purchases are processed by Apple, and the app only knows whether Full Game is active. Both are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing

The game or blitz card only leaves the app when you share or save it.

Children

The app neither asks for nor collects personal data, so it is suitable for any age.
