Privacy Policy for Diedro

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Diedro is a measuring instrument for iPhone: an angle finder that reads the hinge of iPhone Duo, a bubble level, a corner gauge and a true-scale ruler. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it. The short version: nothing you measure leaves your iPhone, and the only thing the app ever sends anywhere is whether Diedro Pro has been purchased.

Hinge angle and motion

On iPhone Duo, Diedro reads the angle of the hinge. While the level or the corner gauge is on screen, it reads the direction of gravity and the orientation of the phone from the motion sensor (gyroscope and accelerometer; never the compass). Both are used only to draw the reading you see, entirely on your device. They are never recorded in the background, transmitted, or shared with the developer or with any third party.

Saved readings and settings

The readings you choose to save, your calibration and your settings are stored locally on your device only. They are not synced to iCloud or to any server and are not accessible to the developer. Deleting the app deletes them.

In-app purchase: Diedro Pro

Diedro Pro is bought through Apple's App Store, either once for good or as a monthly subscription that renews through Apple until you cancel it. Apple processes the payment; Diedro never sees your payment details. To know whether the purchase has been made, and to restore it on a new iPhone, the app uses RevenueCat, a service that checks purchase status with Apple. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt, an anonymous identifier it generates for this installation, and basic device information (device model, OS version, app version, locale). It does not receive your name, email, Apple Account or any reading you take. Diedro does not collect device identifiers such as the advertising identifier for this purpose, and does not use App Tracking Transparency. RevenueCat's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy. On Apple's App Privacy label this appears as "Purchase History", not linked to you.

Accounts and network

Diedro does not require an account. Apart from the purchase check described above, the app makes no network requests. The only links in the app are to this page, the support page and Apple's standard licence terms, and they open in your browser.

Analytics and advertising

Diedro does not use any analytics or advertising SDKs, does not show ads, and does not track you across apps or websites. The only third-party software in the app is the RevenueCat SDK described above.

Children's privacy

Diedro is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children — or from anyone else.

Changes to this policy
