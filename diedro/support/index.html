Diedro Support

Help with the angle finder, level, corner gauge and ruler for iPhone.

Diedro reads an angle from the hinge of iPhone Duo, and is a bubble level, a corner gauge and a true-scale ruler on every iPhone. The answers below cover the most common questions — anything else, email us.

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 27 or later. The hinge tools need iPhone Duo with iOS 27.1 or later; every other iPhone gets the level, the corner gauge and the ruler.

Why do I only see Level, Angle and Rule?

Measure, Square and Target read the angle of the hinge, so they appear only on iPhone Duo. On an iPhone that does not fold, Diedro is a level, a corner gauge and a ruler, and it simulates nothing.

Reading a corner without a hinge

Open Angle. Rest the back of the iPhone flat on one face of the corner and hold it still until it ticks; then rest it on the other face the same way. The turn between the two rests is the corner — two walls, a floor and a wall, the bevel of a blade — and the exterior, supplement and mitre setting come with it. Keep the back flat on the face (a bulky case that rocks will spoil it), and take the second rest within a few seconds of the first: the gyroscope drifts slowly, and the reading is only as good as the two rests. Again starts over.

Why is there no Rule on my iPhone?

The ruler is drawn only on screens whose exact pixel density Diedro knows. On a screen it does not know it shows no ruler, rather than one at the wrong scale. Diedro knows every iPhone screen that had been published when your version came out, iPhone Duo included. If yours shows no Rule, update the app: new iPhone models are added as they are published.

Millimetres or inches?

In Settings, choose the unit of the rule. Inches are marked down to a sixteenth and read as fractions, the way a tape measure is read.

Checking the ruler

A bank card is 85.6 mm long. Lay it along the rule from 0: it should end at the small notch. The Bank card button moves the line there.

Zeroing the level

Lay the iPhone on the surface and tap Zero the level. Turn it half a turn on the same spot and tap again. The two readings cancel the phone's own error, the way a real level is checked. Reset zero undoes it.

Getting a good angle on iPhone Duo

Bring the hinge to the vertex and rest both backs against the faces. The camera stands proud of its half, so press near the bottom edge, never beside the hinge. Hold freezes the reading so you can take the phone off the work; Auto hold does it as soon as the reading is steady.

Does a case change the reading?

On iPhone Duo the angle is the angle between the two backs, so a case of even thickness on both halves does not change it. What spoils a reading is a back that rocks: press near the bottom edge, where the camera does not lift the half off the face. The corner gauge rests the iPhone the same way on both faces, so the camera bump cancels out; a bulky case that rocks on its edges does not.

I hold the phone in my left hand

In Settings, choose which thumb gets Hold. Save moves to the other side.

Calibrating the hinge

In Settings → Calibrate the hinge: open the Duo flat against its stop and record it, then shut it — the closed position is captured by itself, because the screen cannot be seen while shut.

What is Diedro Pro?

On iPhone Duo the live angle, the level and the ruler are free. Diedro Pro unlocks the workshop tools: Hold and Auto hold, the Log, Square, Target, the two-plane level, hinge calibration and the reading on the cover display. You can pay once and keep it for good, or subscribe by the month. Tabs and buttons with a lock mark open the purchase sheet; so does Settings → Diedro Pro. On an iPhone that does not fold there is nothing to unlock: the level, the corner gauge and the ruler are the whole app, and they are free.

How do I cancel the monthly subscription?

It is an Apple subscription, so it is cancelled where all of them are: on your iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Diedro Pro → Cancel, at least a day before it renews. Pro stays on until the end of the month you have paid for, and nothing you saved is lost. The one-time purchase never renews and has nothing to cancel.

Refunds

Apple handles every payment, so refunds are asked for from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot see or refund a purchase ourselves.

"Purchases are not allowed on this iPhone"

Screen Time is blocking in-app purchases. Whoever manages Screen Time on this iPhone can allow In-App Purchases in its App Store restrictions, in Settings.

I bought Pro on another iPhone

The purchase belongs to your Apple Account. On the new iPhone open Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same account. If Family Sharing is on for purchases, family members can restore it too.

Does Diedro need the internet?

Measuring never does. Buying and restoring go through the App Store, so they need a connection.

Languages

Diedro speaks English, Spanish, Catalan, German, French, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Polish, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian and Malay. It follows the language of your iPhone; to choose one for Diedro alone, open Settings → Apps → Diedro → Language.

How accurate is it?

Diedro shows the resolution the sensor really delivers and never adds decimals. Apple guarantees neither the rate nor the precision of the hinge angle, so Diedro is a handy instrument, not a certified one: check anything that matters against a tool you trust.
